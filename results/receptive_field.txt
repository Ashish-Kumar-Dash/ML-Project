Result: the layer mixes time globally and features not at all.
kernel_size=1 constrains the FEATURE axis, because the transpose put
time in the convolution's channel slot. There is no weight sharing
across time and no translation-equivariance: a shifted input produces
an unrelated output, and the weight count is bound to the exact
sequence length the layer was instantiated at.
